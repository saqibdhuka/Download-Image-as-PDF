# Privacy Policy for Download Image as PDF

**Effective Date:** October 1, 2026

## Overview
"Download Image as PDF" (the "Extension") is a browser extension designed to convert web images into PDF documents. We believe in complete user privacy and data security. 

## Data Collection and Usage
**We do not collect, store, or transmit any of your personal data.** 
The Extension operates 100% locally on your device. When you right-click an image and select "Download Image as PDF," the image data is processed entirely within your browser's memory using local scripting and the Offscreen API. 

* **No External Servers:** Your images, browsing history, and URLs are never sent to any external servers or third parties.
* **Host Permissions:** The Extension requires the `<all_urls>` permission strictly to bypass Cross-Origin Resource Sharing (CORS) restrictions, ensuring that images from different websites can be converted locally. It does not use this permission to track your web activity.
* **Active Tab Scripting:** For highly secure sites (like webmail clients), the extension uses active tab scripting to securely copy the image directly from your screen memory. This happens instantly and locally.

## Analytics and Tracking
We do not use any analytics software, tracking cookies, or user profiling.
